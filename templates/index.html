Sorting rules

Each download goes into a folder before it starts. Checks, in order: a channel you've taught it (by moving one of its videos) → a channel listed below → language (e.g. zh also matches Chinese characters in the title) → keywords, scored by where they appear (title and tags count most, the description and first 3 minutes of captions least) → the local AI → the fallback folder. Any subfolder you create in the download folder shows up here too. Channels are the most reliable rule; avoid keywords that are everyday words.
